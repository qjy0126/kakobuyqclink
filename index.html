FAQ

What is Kakobuy QC Link?

An independent catalog that turns spreadsheet-style finds into browsable product cards with QC notes, then sends you to Kakobuy for checkout.

How does Kakobuy fit in?

Kakobuy is the agent — purchase, warehouse, QC, consolidation, and shipping. kakobuyqclink.com only helps you find and open listings.

Are QC photos available?

Many rows include QC reference photos. Warehouse QC from Kakobuy appears after the item arrives at the warehouse.

How much is shipping?

It depends on weight, volume, destination, and line. Use Kakobuy’s estimator after items are in the warehouse.

How long does delivery take?

Many parcels land in 7–20 days after dispatch. Customs and holidays can stretch that.

What if I am unhappy with an item?

Contact Kakobuy support with the order ID and QC photos, ideally while the item is still in the warehouse.
